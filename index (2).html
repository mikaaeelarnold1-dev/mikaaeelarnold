About

Mikaaeel Arnold is an author and poet from the East Rand, South Africa, writing in a dark, gothic and romantic voice, ornate, aching and drenched in crimson moonlight.

Beyond his craft as a creative writer, he is a meticulous editor and proofreader, refining prose with sharp, atmospheric precision. His writing stems from a dark, quiet place within—an inner abyss that has awakened to bleed directly through his ink, giving life to verse and stories born of shadow, beauty, and sorrow.

True Darkness

Oblivion..
A dance caused by trembles beneath my skin
A tale not told but sung with cries
A void filled with empty shreds of hope

My heart shines as a candle in a vast black room
Bound by reality of being changed in the abyss
I found myself breathing in countless shadows
Shadows that filled my lungs with a raging vortex
I remained bright as I burned away
Each passing moment felt nearer to the end
Like a clock that ticked sounds of decay
The end is near and yet life remained in a loop
An endless torture,
Thorns pierced through weeping skin
How wicked could the shadows be
To be shunned and cast away as a forsaken folktale

The tide between countless shadows waged a war
Like an armada the ghosts fled across the room
How could a simple flame breathe through a sea of black?
Alone with only myself to keep me warm
The hands of the abyss clutched onto my neck
And like smoldering rocks despair sank down my throat
A bitter taste of pain stung with bitter haste

My heart afraid of the thread beneath the wax
Wax that covered my heart as gentle walls
Fear lurked through the corners of the abyss
Shatter my bones like a sanctioned mirror
Could I ever see my reflection as a burning flame?
Or would I see a reflection of a passing candle?
Pitch black sorrows may seem true and still
Still as the silence that strayed into the dark
Shimmers of sparks rattle from time to time
It was only a flame burning through the pain

Could a flame shed tears?
Or would those very tears extinguish me?
Only fools had forgotten hopes when nothing feels real
So I remained that fool like a king’s court jester

A friend I never had

Would the world remain vibrant without you?

It was your presence that delivered a warm breeze through the atmosphere—
A presence so intense it chased away the fear.
Your smile, deep as dawn, awakened every living thing within me.
You’re an embodiment of illumination; believe me, you’re the key.

A friend who stood as a knight in shining armour,
A friend who lifted the veil from a dark world without glamour.
A stranger? Once, yes, but eventually manifested as my one and only guardian.
My protector, my elevator, my conqueror; when I shed tears, you’re the one easing my pain.

The pet names you call me are sweet and elegant, yet I weaken every time you say my name.
You’re the one who carried me through tough times, dragging me to the light even when I’d complain.
You remain a stain intertwined with my brain—I wouldn’t want it any other way.
Each time I giggle in a room of silence, I wish you were here.
I wish goodbyes never existed, so you could stay.

A touch of poison that became my obsession

Was my body just another playground?

CONTENT NOTE · Adult themes

What was it that I saw in his crystal blue gaze that had me pulled into a void of lust.
How could a stranger seem familiar? His kindness brushed off like delusions from my past.
A rose he was that I plucked from a field stained in white by a horde of lilies stacked in entity.
Like eye candy, my eyes watered as my lips slipped beneath my teeth, I seemed all willy-nilly.

An addiction that kept me from fading into the howling shadows of the night.
Poison that petrified the urge to resist, temptation stood tall as a lamented blight.
“Mmmm!”, my arms reached out as my hands crawled steadily clinging to the comfort of pleasure.
Pinned down as a slave chained by the cold metals of ecstasy, his sword pierced through my soul without measure.

Like waves that washed over my skin, his touch slithered a faint breeze across my breast.
This was not love and yet he had my heart beating drums behind the cage of my chest.
Like serpents we tangled each other, turning and tossing like raging rapids between the sheets.
It was pure bliss as he held my hand escorting my orgasms through a river that led to my feet.

If I could, I would, another chance is all I need to drown in his poisonous touch.
He tossed me aside as if my body had been forsaken by pleasure-less pleasure, still as a ticking watch.
A man that deemed himself worthy of conquering Eve after devouring her forbidden fruit.
In the end I remained a sandbox for a child to play in, how lust hid behind a deceitful truth.

The cheater that clings to me

Is forgiveness all that we have?

My soul torn to shreds by the very words you left behind.
Abandoned by truth and trust, forsaken in mind.
Blind and yet kind, I try to move on from the chains of regret that kept me confined.
How bonds shattered into a million fragments form a broken mirror—a reflection that couldn’t unwind.

Were my tears not enough? All I ever received were empty sorries filled with mockery.
Anxiety: the only friend that never betrayed me, the only friend that stood beside me.
Each time you smiled, the air filled with miasma, clogging the very breath I seek.
Bleak, wondering if I’d ever hold your hand the same way again—how you left so quick.

Each kiss I received stained my heart with countless threats from another woman’s touch.
It wasn’t much—the love I received whenever you arrived smelling of perfume, it was just a hunch.
And yet, that was a lie I told myself to find the love that once loved me for who I am.
Who’s to blame? Each night I drown in the fear that stained our chamber, but I could never complain.

How could you cherish flowers in a withered garden filled with grey hints of despair?
I do care; I’m only human. Why is it that every time I forgive you, I receive thorns that fill the air?
Why does my heart never learn? Why does my soul cling to false hopes and dreams?
At the end, I could never love another man as much as the one who left a kiss of incompatible compassion—to what means?

How ironic,

But in the end you proved I’m not your missing piece.

It hurts

CONTENT NOTE · Themes of depression and suicide

It hurts ..
It hurts so much.
I blame myself every minute, every second.
Why was it me born in this life?
I hate the person I am.
I hate the way I smile and say it’s ok each time.
I hate the way I tell myself it’s ok.
I hate that…
What am I?
Am I just not meant to be happy?
Why should I feel this way every single day?
Was it fate that brought me here?
Or was I destined to suffer for eternity?

My tears stood frozen glistening through the dark as I stared at shadows that crowd my room.
Every thought betrayed me and every feeling consumed me, maybe it will end very soon.
The white lie that keeps a bladed smile upon my face –
daggers always get the best of me.
Why do I cheer myself with cheers from a crowd-less crowd?
Maybe death would be the key I seek.
Even if I pass the world would remain blissful without me,
with zero worth it’s easy to replace me.
I know I’m not the best and never expected to be a hero,
shamed by destiny to be the villain.

Why can’t I be happy? Is it because the scars ruined the image of my heart?
I barely recognise myself anymore, the countless masks I wear to keep the world happy.
All my life I was a professional actor and yet not once have I received an award.
I just wanted to smile a truthful smile.
I guess some of us aren’t meant to… I get it.
It’s ok, I’ll make it… And yet my tears keep coming back.
Like chains binding my very soul, the tortures that trampled my tepid heart with tyrannical taunts.

Sometimes my tears feel warm and sometimes it feels like a blade slashing down my cheeks.
What is pain to me?
Pain is my friend that never left my side, the one that crushed my hand and suffocated my heart –
Over and over and over again.
Maybe one day I’ll be free from this.
Can I really be free from this pain when every memory haunts me as the demons in my dreams?
Every nightmare felt like never ending lucid dreams – demons that barged in uninvited.
I wish maybe everything could end, and my emptiness from the world place smiles and joys to everyone around.

Could I still be the same without you?

CONTENT NOTE · Themes of self-harm and suicide

Underneath a violet moon twinkled my tears that glimmer as crystals dancing through the air.
Clinging to my heart as it bleeds an endless river, the tighter I hold the more my pain reverts its glare.
A nightly confinement left me dancing with the shadows that I fear – someone save me.
I tried to breathe as my breath betrayed the very lungs that guided my heart to a deep black sea.

The silence behind screams shattered emotions that echoed throughout a void that filled the room.
“Why did it have to be this way?”, my hand choked the very thoughts that left my mind.
They said I was crazy, a lunatic, a girl that lost her way through a realm of forgetting memories.
Maybe I am, maybe those words carried a weight of truth that buried a song without melodies.

How my eyes glistened towards the stars that wish to feel my pain, yet I wished for my end.
“I wish I could be with you”, if only I could cross over the walls of life and death – only a message I wish to send.
As I float over a tepid crimson pool my heart withers away as if autumn entered my very soul.
Currents flowing from my wrist as steady streams washing away the emptiness that once filled my hole.

Spirits that touch the life I once thought was untouchable, the winds called with a lovely cry.
A meditation still and bitter as I share the ground medication that led to temptation – I could fly.
The very air I once knew became a stranger to me, gentle skin that turned pale and cold as ice.
“At least we’ll see each other again”, the last tear drop I felt so warm with life felt so nice.

A rose free as a stolen soul

CONTENT NOTE · Themes of death

It called my name as if fate brought us together.
Panting from the journey that brought me here, my heart beats a calming fear.
Tailed by anxiety and tragic horrors that wish to cage my freedom away.
Should I not have the right to play?
A little girl in a world that seemed gentle in each and every way.
I couldn’t stay.
Ran I did for the beauty that my heart desires, not wealth, not fame, just peace in a little sandbox.

Could I still play?
Why, why, why!
Blood bled a ballet that bestowed a dance banned by brutal behaviour.
How death called for a favour.
All that I had was my smile that kept me going, and here we are in a garden of my passing.
An arrow that pierced through my tiny waist – if only it was Cupid that shot me.
Free at last as I took my last few breaths that held my hand through the dangers clinging to my death.

The sky blissful in every way, leaden grey clouds and a withered sun.
A view that graced my final moments.
Why did it have to end like this?
In a garden of lilies stood one perfect rose, shy and unapologetic crimson through the white.
I laid my head next to it as my blood stained the ground in a sea of red.
I accept this beautiful death, it was as if we were destined to meet.
My last moment with you and yet I wish it could have been our first of many beginnings.

The last sunset that left bitter to my grace

The air crimson in a way and yet stained by citrus outside yellow agenda.
Winds blew a graceful goodbye as the sun found its way in a realm of departure.
A timeless foundation that held day firm as the night began to fall.
An adventure to what felt like forever and yet it was a moment that could last after all.
Birds sang songs that complemented the dancing leaves through the air.
A duet sung by day and night as they exchanged vows – the moon not in sight felt near.

Through it all her hair stood out as a thorn with a flower’s identity, how beauty felt so sad.
Was I mad? How could I be mad at a doll that stood between here and there, gone yet she may stay.
Don’t stray away from peace that held our hearts chained by cold metals of reality.
“Hold me, don’t chase me”, screams that betrayed her gaze as crystals shed down her cheeks.
My eyes complemented the sorrows with a lovely cry, a smile that held my heart warm.
From the darkness that sought to conquer our bonds, blinded by light that took her form.

Alas, what was love in the end when it couldn’t save the hearts of two broken souls.
Why did it feel like a void filled with happiness while I bled shades of despair?
Don’t look at me with a tepid glare, the comfort of your hazel wood left roots of memories beneath my skin.
Never has a simple goodbye felt so aggravating towards my mind as it sparked a dilemma.
As the night came, the abyss of departure sank a rock within my sea of love – I understood and I cared.
Was it me or was it you? How did we end up in a place where dreams and memories roamed the air?

Shadow walker

The shadows beneath my feet

Why hide yourself from the night sky that tossed a veil over a table surrounded by empty chairs?
The abyss bloomed within an hour, time that ticks in shades of neon black star dust.
Why do you pretend to hate the light when you exist through its dwindling aroma?
Or so I say, and yet through the shadows you never existed as if the darkness rejected you from the underworld.

Was it not you that kept me safe when I ran from your threats?
You’ve always been by my side and yet you’ve never been there for me.
An actor who pretended to be my friend on a stage shamed by the blight of a silent audience.
Sing I may, to sway the way you play muted melodies at bay – shattered notes cracked away.

Vows made through birth, a priest of light that chained our paths as one.
Yet we couldn’t touch hands as if we were never meant to be, so you see how it’s always been you and me.
Why do you stare at me as if I’ve lost my sanity to a thief in the winds?
Why do you mimic me? Do you not have a destiny of your own desire?

I tried to let you go to hide the pain away from you, tears that fell from a waterless well.
Breathe as I should, wander as I could, a lost soul roaming around false hope and desire.
Why don’t you break free? Why do you keep your distance, an inch away from my heart?
But at the end my hero, my friend and protector was and always has been my shadow.

A raindance of a broken soul

It was only the rain that understood my pain.

A ballet of rain that danced through the air without a care, a leaden grey sky cried a thousand tears.
Thunder that echoed through the cold, neon veins coloured the sky in crystal blue each time they appear.
The wind whispered a breeze that swayed the trees to lean back as if they gazed upon showers of illusion.
Leaves brushed through the air while the relentless ripples rushed them towards a never ending conclusion.

Through it all I stood still as if time had a moment to cherish my endless sorrows.
The smile that kept me free felt like dilutions devouring my diminished soul – hilarious horrors.
The chills – my skin drowned beneath a sea of droplets, it was as if my body couldn’t breathe through it all.
And yet I danced to note-less notes, a muted melody that played through roars of the fall.

Puddles remained unwearied as I skipped around.
The splashes of each puddle remained anonymous through the rain as they found their way back to the ground.
Around and around I spun, a world that seemed to never end and yet it came to an end.
The clouds held a divorcing ceremony as the rain came to a halt, the rays of light could not pretend.

A rainbow that stood tall in different shades of emotions, how the weather’s personality changed instantly.
From a smile that bloomed in liquid form to a frown that illuminated the sky as if it was meant to be.
Was it really meant to be? How the sun shined upon my hidden tears and revealed the pain within me.
